# Ryan Morris — Staff Software Engineer & Principal Consultant

I am a Staff Software Engineer at AuthSnap and the Principal Consultant at Morris Software LLC, operating as a cross-functional technical generalist and force multiplier across systems architecture, cloud infrastructure, full-stack development, and product execution. Over the course of my career, I have specialized in taking complex distributed systems from early design to resilient production scale—spanning cloud platforms on Azure, high-throughput backend services, secure multi-tenant architectures, modern TypeScript/React interfaces, and automated CI/CD and deployment pipelines.

Much of my recent focus has centered on architecting mission-critical, regulated SaaS platforms in healthcare technology, where data integrity, tenant isolation, and auditability are non-negotiable. I design secure application layers—including zero-trust session management, strict tenancy scoping, and resilient data access patterns—that support clinicians, administrators, and operational teams in high-stakes environments. I frequently bridge the gap between deep systems engineering and business execution, aligning milestone planning and technical roadmaps with the daily realities of end-user workflows.

Across more than a decade of engineering, my work has spanned native mobile development, distributed API design, CMS infrastructure, web accessibility auditing, and modern developer tooling. I approach engineering with a commitment to high-leverage solutions: ruthless simplicity in architectural design, robust automated testing and containerized developer environments, and scalable infrastructure that empowers lean teams to move fast with uncompromising quality.
